## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Five-Elements Skill Casting, 64-Direction Projectiles & Combat Resolution (Priority: P1)

A player or autonomous bot outside a peaceful town zone (`FightMode` enabled) casts a learned martial arts skill or basic attack at either a locked target entity or a ground coordinate, or switches their active right-hand aura skill. The server verifies the action's security check value, fight-mode eligibility, cooldown timer, range, and vital costs (mana/stamina), broadcasts the skill cast animation to all observers in the surrounding 3x3 region Area of Interest (AOI), spawns and advances any resulting projectiles along the authentic 64-direction trigonometric trajectory each 18-Hz simulation tick, resolves hit collisions against valid hostile targets, calculates Five-Elements damage, resistances, and status ailments (freeze/slow, poison damage-over-time, stun), broadcasts hurt reactions, and updates target vitals in real time.

**Why this priority**: Real-time martial arts skill execution, 64-direction projectile physics, and Five-Elements damage resolution form the core gameplay loop of Võ Lâm Truyền Kỳ and are required before any monster hunting or player-versus-player combat can occur.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by moving a character out of Ba Lăng Huyện through a town gate into combat mode, casting both melee and ranged projectile skills at a nearby monster or hostile player, and verifying that surrounding observers see the skill cast animation, projectile flight, hurt reaction, and accurate health reduction while mana/stamina costs and skill cooldowns are enforced on the caster.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an in-world character in combat mode (`FightMode = 1`) who knows an active skill and has sufficient mana/stamina, **When** the client submits a valid skill command (`0x4D` or wrapped auto-attack `0x87`) targeting an in-range hostile entity or ground coordinate, **Then** the server deducts the required vital costs, starts the skill cooldown, broadcasts the skill cast notification (`0x5A`) to the 3x3 region neighborhood, and spawns the skill's melee hitbox or 64-direction projectile instances.
2. **Given** one or more active skill projectiles traveling across the map on the 18-Hz simulation loop, **When** a projectile steps along its 64-direction velocity vector and collides with a valid hostile target within its hit radius (or expires at maximum range/obstacle), **Then** the server computes Five-Elements physical and elemental damage mitigated by the defender's resistances, applies any freeze/slow, poison, or stun status effects, broadcasts the hurt command (`0x56` with `do_hurt`) to the 3x3 region neighborhood, and updates the defender's health.
3. **Given** a character standing inside a peaceful zone (`FightMode = 0`), lacking sufficient mana/stamina, on skill cooldown, attempting to cast an unlearned skill, or submitting an invalid security check value, **When** the client sends a skill cast command, **Then** the server rejects the cast without deducting vitals or spawning projectiles.
4. **Given** a character who has learned an aura skill, **When** the client sends a change aura skill command (`0x6F`), **Then** the server validates ownership of the skill and updates the character's active aura state.

### User Story 2 - Monster AI, Death, Experience Progression & Revival (Priority: P2)

Field monsters spawned around starter villages and hunting grounds autonomously patrol near their home coordinates, acquire and pursue hostile targets that enter their aggro range (or attack them), execute attacks when within range, and retreat to their spawn origin if pulled beyond their leash radius. When a monster is slain, the server broadcasts its death, awards experience to the killer (advancing the character's level and granting unallocated attribute and skill points when crossing level thresholds, which the player can allocate into base attributes and skills), clears the monster from the active region until its respawn timer elapses, and respawns it at full health. When a player is slain, the server broadcasts the player's death and allows the player to revive at their saved town revival point.

**Why this priority**: Autonomous monster behavior, death/respawn cycles, experience gain, level-ups, and player revival complete the fundamental PVE progression loop for both human players and autonomous farming bots.

**Independent Test**: Can be tested independently by having a character or bot engage and kill field monsters outside Ba Lăng Huyện (Map `53`), verifying that monsters fight back and pursue the player, broadcast death (`0x57`) upon reaching zero health, grant experience and level-up attribute/skill points that can be allocated (`0x57` / `0x58`), and respawn at their home coordinates after the respawn delay, as well as verifying that a defeated player revives cleanly in town (`0x74` $\rightarrow$ `0x89`).

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a living field monster in an active map region, **When** simulation ticks advance, **Then** the monster periodically patrols within its home radius, switches to pursuing and attacking a hostile player who attacks it or enters its aggro radius, and returns to its home spawn point if the target dies, leaves the map, enters a peaceful zone, or exceeds the monster's pursuit leash distance.
2. **Given** a monster whose health is reduced to zero by a player's attack, **When** the fatal hit is resolved, **Then** the server transitions the monster to dead state, broadcasts the death notification (`0x57`) to the 3x3 region neighborhood, awards experience to the attacking player, and schedules the monster to respawn at its original spawn coordinates with full health after its configured respawn duration.
3. **Given** a player who gains enough experience from slaying monsters to reach the next character level threshold, **When** the experience is applied, **Then** the character's level increases, maximum health/mana/stamina increase according to their five-elements class growth rates, unallocated base attribute points (`Tiềm năng`, 5 per level) and skill points (`Kỹ năng`, 1 per level) are granted, and the updated character stats are synchronized to the client.
4. **Given** a player with unallocated attribute points or skill points, **When** the client requests base attribute allocation (`0x57` for Strength, Dexterity, Vitality, or Energy) or skill point allocation (`0x58` for a learned skill), **Then** the server validates available points, increments the requested attribute or skill level, recalculates derived combat and vital statistics, and sends the attribute sync (`0x5D`) or skill level sync (`0x5E`) back to the client.
5. **Given** a player whose health is reduced to zero in combat, **When** the fatal hit occurs and the client subsequently sends a player revive request (`0x74`), **Then** the server broadcasts the death notification (`0x57`) on death and, upon receiving the revive request, restores the player's vitals, resets `FightMode` to peaceful (`0`), teleports the player to their saved town revival coordinates, and broadcasts the revival notification (`0x89`) and position sync.

### User Story 3 - Monster Loot Drops, 6-Affix Blue Equipment Generation & Ground Objects (Priority: P3)

When a monster dies, the server evaluates the monster's drop-rate table to generate ground loot objects—including currency (gold/lượng), healing/mana potions, town portal scrolls, and randomized equipment (White gear and 6-affix Blue equipment with up to 3 explicit and 3 five-elements-activated hidden magic attributes)—and places them on walkable cells around the slain monster. Players within the 3x3 region neighborhood immediately see the dropped items and currency on the ground (`0x64` `s2c_objadd`), and the server removes expired or picked-up ground objects (`0x67` `s2c_objremove`) from observers' views.

**Why this priority**: Rewarding monster kills with gold, potions, and randomized 6-affix Blue equipment is the core itemization pillar of Võ Lâm Truyền Kỳ and provides the supplies needed for sustained hunting.

**Independent Test**: Can be tested by defeating monsters configured with drop tables in Map `53`, verifying that gold and items spawn on nearby walkable cells and appear on connected clients via `0x64`, verifying that generated Blue equipment carries valid Five-Elements series, level, and up to 6 magic attribute slots, and confirming that uncollected ground objects despawn after their expiration timer via `0x67`.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a monster slain by a player, **When** the monster's drop-rate rules trigger currency or item drops, **Then** the server generates the corresponding ground objects (including assigning a globally unique item identity and rolling up to 3 explicit and 3 hidden magic attributes for Blue equipment based on item level, luck, and Five-Elements series), places them on valid walkable coordinates near the death location, and broadcasts ground object addition (`0x64`) to all players in the 3x3 region neighborhood.
2. **Given** a player entering a new region or moving across a region boundary, **When** the newly entered regions contain active ground objects, **Then** the server synchronizes all existing ground objects (`0x64`) in those newly entered regions to the player (and responds to explicit object detail queries `0x4A` / clicks `0x72`).
3. **Given** a ground object dropped from a monster kill, **When** a non-owner player attempts to pick it up during the initial killer-protection window, **Then** the server denies the pickup until the protection timer expires; and when the ground object's total lifetime expires without being picked up, the server removes it from the region and broadcasts object removal (`0x67` with reason `0`) to the 3x3 region neighborhood.

### User Story 4 - 6x10 Inventory Grid, Equipment Wearing, Potion Consumption & Town Portal (Priority: P4)

Players and autonomous bots pick up ground gold and items (`0x5A`) into their `6x10` main equipment bag or quick-use belt, receive complete item synchronization (`0x5F`) and gold balance synchronization (`0x61`), move or swap items between their bag, cursor hand, quick-use belt, and equipment slots (`0x5B` $\rightarrow$ `0x61` + `0x8E`), drop unwanted items onto the ground (`0x5E` / `0x83`), split stackable items (`0x85`), equip weapons and armor to boost combat attributes and activate Five-Elements hidden affixes, consume healing/mana medicines (`0x59`) to recover vitals over time, and use Town Portal scrolls (`Thổ Địa Phù` via `0x59` or `0x87` sub-command `0x02`) to teleport back to their town revival point.

**Why this priority**: Managing a spatial 2D inventory grid (`width x height` item footprints), equipping dropped weapons/armor, drinking potions during combat, and using Town Portal scrolls are essential for character survival, combat scaling, and autonomous bot operation.

**Independent Test**: Can be tested by having a character pick up gold, potions, a Town Portal scroll, and a piece of Blue equipment from the ground, verifying 2D grid placement in the `6x10` bag, moving the equipment to the character's equipment slot (`pos_equip`) and verifying updated character stats, consuming a potion (`0x59`) to observe vital regeneration and item removal (`0x60`), and activating a Town Portal scroll to return to town.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a character standing within pickup range of a ground object (`0x64`), **When** the client sends a pickup command (`0x5A`), **Then** for gold objects the server increments the character's inventory gold balance, sends the money sync packet (`0x68` `s2c_syncmoney`), and broadcasts ground object removal (`0x67` with reason `1`); for item objects the server validates free `width x height` space in the character's `6x10` bag (or quick-use belt), transfers the item into the inventory, sends the 80-byte item sync packet (`0x5F`), and broadcasts ground object removal (`0x67`).
2. **Given** a character holding items in their inventory containers (`pos_hand`, `pos_equip`, `pos_equiproom`, `pos_immediacy`), **When** the client sends an item move command (`0x5B`) to place, move, or swap an item, **Then** the server validates container bounds, item footprint collisions (`width x height`), and equipment slot requirements (part type, gender, series, level, and base attribute requirements when equipping into `pos_equip`), updates the item's placement, recalculates character combat stats and Five-Elements hidden affix activations if equipment changed, and responds with the move confirmation (`0x61` `s2c_playermoveitem`) and auto-move unlock (`0x8E`).
3. **Given** a character possessing a healing/mana medicine or Town Portal scroll in their bag (`pos_equiproom`) or belt (`pos_immediacy`), **When** the client sends an eat/use item command (`0x59`, or auto-attack emergency town portal `0x87` sub-command `0x02`), **Then** the server consumes one charge/unit of the item (decrementing stack count or sending item removal `0x60`), applies the medicine's gradual health/mana restoration over its configured duration frames or teleports the character to their saved town revival point in peaceful mode, and synchronizes updated vitals/position.
4. **Given** a character holding an item in their cursor hand (`pos_hand`) or carrying medicines in their bag, **When** the client sends a throw-away item command (`0x5E`) or throw-away-all-medicine command (`0x83`), **Then** the server removes the item(s) from the character's inventory (`0x60`) and spawns the corresponding ground object(s) (`0x64`) at the character's feet.
5. **Given** a character logging into the world or disconnecting, **When** world entry synchronization or session save occurs, **Then** all owned inventory and equipped items (along with their unique item identifiers, durability, stack counts, and 6 magic attributes) and gold balances are loaded, synchronized (`0x5F`, `0x68`), and durably persisted.

### User Story 5 - Dual-Tier Canonical Game Data (`JXData Standard v1`) & Offline Ground-Truth Export (Priority: P5)

An offline CLI tool (`tools/JXServer.DataExporter`) extracts original maps, player progression tables, NPC/monster templates, skill/missile definitions (including evaluated per-level scaling curves from `script/skill/**/*.lua`), item/magic-affix catalogs (`item/004/*.txt`), and monster drop-rate tables (`droprate/*.ini`) from `jx-linux-bin/server1` into schema-validated UTF-8 JSON documents under `data/` (`maps/`, `players/`, `npcs/`, `skills/`, `items/`, `loot/`) and compiles them into `MemoryPack` snapshots (`artifacts/*.pack`). On server startup, `JXServer.GameAssets` loads exclusively from the `MemoryPack` snapshot (or `data/**/*.json` in development) into immutable in-memory catalogs (`IMapCatalog`, `IPlayerTemplateCatalog`, `INpcTemplateCatalog`, `ISkillTemplateCatalog`, `IMissileTemplateCatalog`, `IItemTemplateCatalog`, `ILootTableCatalog`).

**Why this priority**: Transitioning `JXServer.GameAssets` to the Dual-Tier `JXData Standard v1` (`data/**/*.json` + `MemoryPack`) eliminates runtime dependency on fragmented legacy `.txt`/`.ini`/`.dat`/`.lua` files while preserving 100% mathematical and wire-protocol fidelity.

**Independent Test**: Can be tested by running `tools/JXServer.DataExporter` against `jx-linux-bin/server1` to verify that all generated `data/**/*.json` files pass validation against `schemas/*.schema.json`, and verifying through catalog unit tests that `JXServer.GameAssets` loads the JSON and `MemoryPack` catalogs accurately with localized UTF-8 Vietnamese names.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the canonical `data/` directory (or compiled `artifacts/*.pack` snapshot), **When** the server initializes, **Then** `JXServer.GameAssets` loads and indexes maps, player progression tables, NPC/monster templates, skill definitions, missile definitions, ground object templates, monster loot tables, and item/magic-attribute catalogs into read-only in-memory `I*Catalog` registries with zero legacy file I/O.
2. **Given** an NPC template in `data/npcs/*.npcs.json` and a map spawn group in `data/maps/*.map.json`, **When** a monster spawns in a map zone, **Then** the zone links the monster instance to its resolved combat skills, level-scaled combat stats (`combatScaling`), attack range, experience value, and drop-rate profile (`lootTableCode`).

### Edge Cases

- **Concurrent Pickup Race on the Same Ground Object**: Two players or bots in the same region send a pickup command (`0x5A`) for the exact same ground item or gold pile on the exact same simulation tick; because the map zone processes commands sequentially on a single-threaded 18-Hz loop, the first command claims the object and removes it from the region while the second command safely sees the object no longer exists, preventing item or currency duplication.
- **Inventory Full During Ground Item Pickup**: A player attempts to pick up a `2x3` armor piece from the ground when their `6x10` bag has no contiguous `2x3` free rectangle; the server leaves the ground object untouched on the map and notifies the player that their inventory is full without losing the item.
- **Target Dies or Leaves Map While Projectiles Are Mid-Flight**: A ranged projectile is flying toward a target that dies from another hit or teleports out of the map before impact; on subsequent ticks the projectile safely skips dead/absent targets and terminates cleanly without null-reference errors or duplicate death triggers.
- **High-Density Projectile Burst in a Single Region**: 50 players and monsters simultaneously cast multi-projectile skills in the same region; projectiles are managed in pre-allocated zone slots with bounded lifetime frames so tick latency remains within the 18-Hz budget without garbage-collection spikes.
- **Character Disconnects While Holding an Item on the Cursor Hand (`pos_hand`)**: A player disconnects or logs out while an item is temporarily lifted into `pos_hand`; the server persists `pos_hand` (or restores it into the character's inventory) so the item is never lost on reconnect.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: On startup, `JXServer.GameAssets` MUST load and index static world, combat, and itemization catalogs exclusively from the Dual-Tier `JXData Standard v1` (`artifacts/*.pack` `MemoryPack` snapshots or schema-validated UTF-8 JSON documents under `data/`): unified maps (`data/maps/*.map.json`), player progression and Five-Elements matrices (`data/players/*.players.json`), NPC/monster templates and level-scaling curves (`data/npcs/*.npcs.json`), skill templates, 3-parameter per-level scaling curves, and projectile templates (`data/skills/*.skills.json`), equipment/medicine/quest items, per-part magic affix tables, and ground object templates (`data/items/*.items.json`), and weighted monster loot tables (`data/loot/*.loot.json`), while `tools/JXServer.DataExporter` extracts and transcodes these canonical JSON catalogs offline from `jx-linux-bin/server1`.
- **FR-002**: The world server MUST process skill execution commands (`0x4D` `c2s_npcskill`, 17 bytes, validated with `IsCheckValueValid` key `0x3AB9`) and auto-attack envelope commands (`0x87` `c2s_autoattacknpc`, 26 bytes, supporting sub-command `0x02` emergency town portal and `0x03` wrapped walk `0x4B` / skill `0x4D`), supporting both ground-coordinate targeting (`nMpsX >= 0`, `nMpsY`) and locked-entity targeting (`nMpsX == -1`, `nMpsYOrTargetID == TargetNpcID`), as well as right-hand aura skill selection (`0x6F` `c2s_changeauraskill`, 5 bytes).
- **FR-003**: Before executing a skill cast, the zone engine MUST verify that the caster is alive, not stunned/frozen from casting, in a combat-enabled state (`FightMode == 1` for offensive skills), owns the requested skill (or is a monster using its template skill), has completed the skill's cooldown (`WaitTime` frames), has sufficient mana/stamina, and is within the skill's effective cast range (`AttackRadius`), broadcasting `s2c_skillcast` (`0x5A`, 25 bytes, or `0x85` `s2c_castskilldirectly` for direct/instant casts) to all players in the 3x3 region AOI upon acceptance.
- **FR-004**: The zone engine MUST simulate active projectiles (missiles) deterministically on the 18-Hz tick loop using the canonical 64-direction fixed-point trigonometric tables (`g_nSin` / `g_nCos` scaled by `1024`), advancing projectile coordinates each tick, checking obstacle collisions and target hitboxes within the surrounding regions, and expiring projectiles when their lifetime frames (`LifeTime`) elapse or when non-piercing projectiles strike a target.
- **FR-005**: When a melee skill frame or projectile collides with a valid hostile target (evaluated via the `KNpcSet::GetRelation` matrix from `jx-win-src`—requiring `FightMode == 1`, non-`DialogNpc` kind, and `relation_enemy` across monster camps or player normal PK flags toggled via `0x76` `c2s_pkapplychangenormalflag`), the combat engine MUST compute damage using the Five-Elements (`Ngũ Hành`: Metal $\rightarrow$ Wood $\rightarrow$ Earth $\rightarrow$ Water $\rightarrow$ Fire $\rightarrow$ Metal) damage and resistance formulas, apply elemental status effects (Cold slow duration, Poison periodic damage per tick, Lightning stun duration), deduct target health, broadcast `s2c_broadcasenetcommand` (`0x56`, 22 bytes, `CmdKind = 9` `do_hurt`) when hurt animation triggers, and synchronize updated vitals (`0x46` for the affected player, `0x4D` compact life percentage for surrounding observers).
- **FR-006**: The zone engine MUST execute a deterministic 18-Hz AI state machine (`Idle/Patrol` $\rightarrow$ `Aggro/Pursuit` $\rightarrow$ `Attack` $\rightarrow$ `Leash/Return` $\rightarrow$ `Dead/RespawnWait`) for all combat monsters (`EntityKind.Monster`), causing monsters to patrol near their spawn point, pursue and attack hostile targets within aggro/attack range, return to their spawn point if pulled beyond their maximum pursuit distance, and respawn at their original spawn coordinates with full vitals after their configured respawn delay following death.
- **FR-007**: When a monster's health reaches `0`, the zone engine MUST transition the monster to dead state, broadcast `s2c_npcdeath` (`0x57`, 5 bytes) to the 3x3 region AOI, grant experience points to the killer (or party/top damage contributor), evaluate level-up thresholds (incrementing character level, granting `5` unallocated attribute points and `1` unallocated skill point per level, increasing max vitals per `level_add.txt`, and syncing `0x44`/`0x46`), and trigger the monster's loot drop evaluation.
- **FR-008**: The world server MUST process player base attribute point allocation (`0x57` `c2s_playeraddbaseattribute`, 14 bytes $\rightarrow$ `0x5D` `s2c_playerattribute`, 14 bytes) for Strength (`0`), Dexterity (`1`), Vitality (`2`), and Energy (`3`), and skill point allocation (`0x58` `c2s_playerapplyaddskillpoint`, 17 bytes $\rightarrow$ `0x5E` `s2c_playerskilllevel`, 15 bytes), validating unallocated point balances and recalculating derived character vitals and combat statistics.
- **FR-009**: When a player's health reaches `0`, the zone engine MUST transition the player to dead posture, clear active hostile monster aggro against that player, and broadcast `s2c_npcdeath` (`0x57`, 5 bytes) to the 3x3 region AOI. Upon receiving `c2s_playerrevive` (`0x74`, 13 bytes), the server MUST restore the player's vitals, set `FightMode = 0`, move the player to their saved revival map and coordinates (`RevivalMapId`, `RevivalPointId`), and broadcast `s2c_npcrevive` (`0x89`, 6 bytes) along with map/position synchronization.
- **FR-010**: The item generator MUST follow `KItemGenerator.cpp` and `KItemSet.cpp` from `jx-win-src` with `m_wVersion = 4` using the canonical `data/items/*.items.json` catalog (exported from `settings/item/004/*.txt`), supporting instantiating currency drops, stackable medicines, town portal scrolls, white equipment, and randomized 6-affix Blue equipment (`Quality = 0` normal/blue with up to 3 explicit prefix/suffix affixes and 3 hidden Five-Elements affixes rolled from `magicAffixes` according to equipment part weight, item series, level, and player luck), assigning each non-stackable item a globally unique `ItemId` (`Guid`) in the domain/persistence layer alongside a runtime uint32 `m_ID` for client wire packets, and supporting configurable starter consumable seeding (`starterItems`: healing/mana potions and a Town Portal scroll in `pos_equiproom` / `pos_immediacy`) upon character creation.
- **FR-011**: Ground objects (dropped items and gold) MUST be managed within their spatial region, synchronized to entering and nearby players via `s2c_objadd` (`0x64`, `35 + NameLen` bytes) and `s2c_objremove` (`0x67`, 6 bytes), respond to `c2s_requestobj` (`0x4A`, 5 bytes) and `c2s_objmouseclick` (`0x72`, 9 bytes), enforce a configurable killer-ownership protection window before becoming public, and automatically expire after their ground lifetime.
- **FR-012**: The server MUST maintain each character's spatial inventory across standard containers (`pos_hand = 1`, `pos_equip = 2` with 15 equipment parts, `pos_equiproom = 3` `6x10` grid, and `pos_immediacy = 7` quick-use belt), synchronizing all owned items (`0x5F` `s2c_syncitem`, 80 bytes, including any item in `pos_hand`) and currency balances (`0x68` `s2c_syncmoney`, 13 bytes) upon world entry and whenever items or gold change.
- **FR-013**: The world server MUST process item pickup (`0x5A` `c2s_playerpickupitem`, 12 bytes), item grid movement/equipping/swapping (`0x5B` `c2s_playermoveitem`, 7 bytes $\rightarrow$ `0x61` `s2c_playermoveitem`, 7 bytes + `0x8E` `s2c_ItemAutoMove`, 1 byte), item dropping (`0x5E` `c2s_playerthrowawayitem`, 1 byte and `0x83` `c2s_throwawayallmedicine`, 4 bytes), stack splitting (`0x85` `c2s_playerdivideitem`, 10 bytes), and item consumption (`0x59` `c2s_playereatitem`, 16 bytes $\rightarrow$ `0x60` `s2c_removeitem`, 5 bytes when depleted), recalculating the character's equipped stats and Five-Elements hidden affix activation chain (Helm/Foot $\leftarrow$ Weapon/Amulet $\leftarrow$ Body/Neck $\leftarrow$ Belt/Wind $\leftarrow$ Cuff/Pendant $\leftarrow$ Ring) whenever equipped gear changes.
- **FR-014**: Character progression (level, experience, allocated attributes, skill levels, current vitals), gold balances, and all owned inventory/equipped items (including their grid coordinates, durability, stack counts, random seeds, and 6 magic attributes) MUST be durably persisted via the control-plane persistence layer on logout, map transfer, and periodic/critical state updates under lease-epoch fencing.

### Key Entities *(include if feature involves data)*

- **SkillTemplate**: Represents a pre-loaded martial arts skill definition from `data/skills/*.skills.json`, including skill ID, `kebab-case` code, localized UTF-8 name, skill style (`melee`, `projectile`, `selfBuff`, `aura`, `passive`, etc.), elemental series, cast range (`CastRangeMps`), mana/stamina cost (`CostScaling`), cooldown frames (`WaitFrames`), projectile reference (`MissileId`), child/event skill references, and 3-parameter per-level `EffectScaling` curves.
- **MissileTemplate**: Represents a pre-loaded projectile archetype from `data/skills/*.skills.json`, defining movement pattern (`line64`, `spread`, `circle`, `follow`, `parabola`, etc.), speed (`SpeedMpsPerFrame`), lifetime frames (`LifeFrames`), hit radius (`HitRadiusMps`), damage interval frames, and pierce/explosion behavior.
- **ActiveMissileSlot**: Represents a live in-flight projectile instance inside a map zone's 18-Hz simulation pool, tracking its caster runtime ID, skill ID/level, current world coordinates (`MpsX`, `MpsY`), 64-direction velocity vector (`DirIndex`, `StepX`, `StepY`), remaining lifetime frames, and pre-computed Five-Elements damage payload.
- **ItemTemplate & MagicAttributeTemplate**: Represent static equipment, medicine, and magic affix definitions loaded from `data/items/*.items.json`, defining item genre, detail type, particular type, quality (`0..4`), grid dimensions (`GridWidthCells`, `GridHeightCells`), `AppearanceResId`, base requirements, base defense/damage ranges, and magic attribute roll weights by equipment part (`WeightsByEquipPart`), Five-Elements series, and level.
- **CharacterItem**: Represents a persisted item instance owned by a character, holding a globally unique `Guid` identity, item classification (`Genre`, `DetailType`, `ParticularType`, `Quality`), Five-Elements `Series`, `Level`, `Luck`, `RandomSeed`, stack count, durability (`CurrentDurability`, `MaxDurability`, `IsBroken`), binding/expiration state, container placement (`ContainerPlace`, `GridX`, `GridY`), and up to 6 magic attribute slots (`AttributeId`, `Value0`, `Value1`, `Value2`, `EnchaseLevel`).
- **GroundObjectSlot**: Represents a live item or gold pile lying on the ground inside a map region, tracking its runtime `ObjId`, world coordinates (`MpsX`, `MpsY`), gold amount or `CharacterItem` payload, owner character ID, ownership protection expiry tick, and despawn expiry tick.
